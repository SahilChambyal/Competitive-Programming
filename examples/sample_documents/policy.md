## Leave Application Process

### Types of Leave
1. **Annual Leave**: Up to 21 days per year
2. **Sick Leave**: Up to 10 days per year with medical certificate
3. **Emergency Leave**: Up to 3 days per year for family emergencies

### Application Process
1. Fill out Form-123 (Leave Application Form)
2. Get supervisor approval
3. Submit to HR department at least 7 days in advance
4. Receive confirmation via email

### Required Documents
- Leave application form (Form-123)
- Medical certificate (for sick leave)
- Supporting documents (for emergency leave)

## Academic Policies (For Educational Institutions)

### Attendance Requirements
- Minimum 75% attendance required for exam eligibility
- Medical leave with proper documentation doesn't count against attendance
- Late arrival (more than 15 minutes) counts as half absence

### Grading System
- A+: 90-100%
- A: 80-89%
- B+: 70-79%
- B: 60-69%
- C: 50-59%
- F: Below 50% (Fail)

### Examination Rules
1. Students must arrive 30 minutes before exam time
2. Valid ID required for entry
3. No electronic devices allowed
4. Cheating results in immediate disqualification

## Document Request Process

### Available Documents
- Transcripts
- Employment certificates
- Tax documents
- Medical records (where applicable)

### Request Process
1. Fill out Document Request Form (Form-456)
2. Pay applicable fees
3. Processing time: 3-5 business days
4. Collection or delivery as per preference

### Fees Structure
- Official transcripts: $25
- Employment certificates: $15
- Expedited processing: Additional $10
